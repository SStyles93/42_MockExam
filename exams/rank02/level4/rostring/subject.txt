Assignment name  : rostring
Expected files   : rostring.c
Allowed functions: write, malloc, free
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that takes a string and displays it rotated by one word to the
left: the first word becomes the last one, and the other words keep their
order. Words are separated by a single space in the output, which is followed
by a newline.

A word is a sequence of characters delimited by spaces, tabs, or the beginning
or end of the string.

If there is no argument, display only a newline. If there are several
arguments, only the first one is used.

Examples:

$> ./rostring "one two three" | cat -e
two three one$
$> ./rostring "   spaced    out   words " | cat -e
out words spaced$
$> ./rostring "solo   " | cat -e
solo$
$> ./rostring "first" "ignored" | cat -e
first$
$> ./rostring | cat -e
$
