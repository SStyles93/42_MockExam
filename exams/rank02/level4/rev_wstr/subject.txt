Assignment name  : rev_wstr
Expected files   : rev_wstr.c
Allowed functions: write, malloc, free
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that takes a string and displays its words in reverse order,
separated by single spaces, followed by a newline.

A word is a sequence of characters delimited by spaces, tabs, or the beginning
or end of the string. The tested strings have no leading or trailing blanks,
and words are separated by exactly one space.

If the number of arguments is not 1, display only a newline.

Examples:

$> ./rev_wstr "the last shall be first" | cat -e
first be shall last the$
$> ./rev_wstr "alone" | cat -e
alone$
$> ./rev_wstr | cat -e
$
