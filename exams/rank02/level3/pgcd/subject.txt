Assignment name  : pgcd
Expected files   : pgcd.c
Allowed functions: printf, atoi, malloc, free
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that takes two strings representing strictly positive integers
that fit in an int, and displays their greatest common divisor followed by a
newline (it is always a strictly positive integer).

If the number of arguments is not 2, display only a newline.

Examples:

$> ./pgcd 48 36 | cat -e
12$
$> ./pgcd 17 5 | cat -e
1$
$> ./pgcd 100 100 | cat -e
100$
$> ./pgcd 42 | cat -e
$
