Assignment name  : add_prime_sum
Expected files   : add_prime_sum.c
Allowed functions: write, exit
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a program that takes a positive integer and displays the sum of all the
prime numbers lower than or equal to it, followed by a newline.

If the number of arguments is not 1, or if the argument is not a positive
number, display 0 followed by a newline.

Examples:

$> ./add_prime_sum 10 | cat -e
17$
$> ./add_prime_sum 2 | cat -e
2$
$> ./add_prime_sum 1 | cat -e
0$
$> ./add_prime_sum | cat -e
0$
