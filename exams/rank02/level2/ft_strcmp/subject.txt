Assignment name  : ft_strcmp
Expected files   : ft_strcmp.c
Allowed functions: 
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Reproduce the behavior of the standard strcmp function (man strcmp): compare
the strings s1 and s2 and return a negative, zero or positive value depending
on whether s1 is lower than, equal to, or greater than s2.

Your function must be declared as follows:

int	ft_strcmp(char *s1, char *s2);
