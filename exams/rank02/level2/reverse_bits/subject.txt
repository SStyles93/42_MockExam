Assignment name  : reverse_bits
Expected files   : reverse_bits.c
Allowed functions: 
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a function that takes a byte, reverses the order of its bits (the most
significant bit becomes the least significant one, and so on) and returns the
result.

Your function must be declared as follows:

unsigned char	reverse_bits(unsigned char octet);

Example:

  0010 0110   (38)
      ||
      \/
  0110 0100   (100)
