Assignment name  : swap_bits
Expected files   : swap_bits.c
Allowed functions: 
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Write a function that takes a byte, swaps its two halves (the 4 high bits and
the 4 low bits) and returns the result.

Your function must be declared as follows:

unsigned char	swap_bits(unsigned char octet);

Example:

  0100 | 0001   (65)
      \ /
      / \
  0001 | 0100   (20)
